  Coupling: the rules read 188 type names. 8 are not in the sqlfluff 4.4.0
  grammar and 54 are in a T3 rename group. 66 of 77 rules read a name that
  differs. 73 of 77 rules see a changed tree if sqruff gives sqlfluff trees
  (not CV09, JJ01, LT12, ST01). Estimate: the names of the helper modules
  come from the `use` items, not from the calls.
